Exhibit Two

The Five Bugs

Every honest data project has a bug section. Click each one. They get worse before they get better.

The Null Model Bug CRITICAL

Every model returned 47.8% accuracy. Tuning made no difference. Features made no difference.

The cause: tune_grid() with mn_log_loss selected penalty = 4.0 — a 4,000× our intended range. Every coefficient was zero. The model predicted 0.487 for every game. The 47.8% was the base rate.

Fix: direct glmnet with fixed lambda. Suddenly higher confidence predicted higher accuracy. Lesson: log loss rewards null models in low-signal domains.

79 Postgame Leakers DATA LEAK

Our feature store contained 79 columns computed from the same game's play-by-play. They had correlations of 0.50–0.68 with the target. The model looked exceptional in development and would have been useless in production.

Fix: strict whitelist. Only roll3/roll5 from prior games admitted.

The Spread Was Negative SIGN ERROR

spread_fav ranged [-26.5, -1.0]. We had been modeling the spread with the wrong sign for weeks.

Fix: mutate(spread_fav = abs(spread_fav)).

Python EPA Was Upside Down SIGN ERROR

R-calculated EPA and Python-calculated EPA correlated at -0.97. They were measuring the same thing from opposite perspectives.

Fix: auto-detected the sign flip, negated all _diff columns from the Python store.

37 Flippy-Floppy Features CALIBRATION

A "flippy" feature predicts covers in one era and no-covers in another. We found 37 of them. Used in an ensemble, they made the model anti-calibrated — its highest-confidence picks were its least reliable.

Fix: identified all 37 by checking sign across training windows. Excluded them. Used only the 229 stable features.

Exhibit Eight

The Honest Assessment

We built something that may have a real edge. Or we may have gotten lucky for three years. Both are still on the table.

✓ What we know
The methodology is clean. Train/test splits are legitimate. All 5 bugs were found and fixed. The 90% CI [52.5%, 59.4%] clears the breakeven threshold. Both cover and no-cover sleeves add independent value. The 2025 holdout — never seen during development — was profitable.
✗ What we cannot claim
Statistical significance at p<0.05. The binomial p-value is 0.057 — marginal. Three NFL seasons (520 bets) is not enough to rule out luck at this win rate. The lower bound of the CI (52.5%) barely clears breakeven. Many strategies with similar 3-year track records eventually regress.
Recommendation: Track the strategy live for 2–3 more seasons before staking real capital. The signal is real enough to watch. Not certain enough to bet a retirement fund on.
